# Prompt review (2026-11-23)

Review of the 127 reflection and prayer prompts drafted for the 128 references added in commit 27ddf28 (Isaiah 55:8-9 serves two feelings). Each was read against its WEB text and its feeling, and checked for pastoral tone, theological care, and fit with the original prompts' voice.

46 references changed (53 fields). The other 81 were left as drafted. The most common fix: a prayer that recited the verse, which the app already shows, now starts from how the reader feels, as the original prompts do.

## Consider swapping

Prompts for these were kept gentle, but the verse's fit with its feeling is doubtful. Not swapped.

- **Matthew 6:14-15** (Angry / Resentful): conditional forgiveness can land as a threat on someone still hurting. Colossians 3:13 or Ephesians 4:32 may fit better.
- **Matthew 7:7-8** (Doubtful / Unanswered prayer): "everyone who asks receives" can sting when a prayer seems unanswered.
- **Habakkuk 2:3** (Hopeful / Waiting on God): about one specific vision of judgment, not personal hopes.
- **Isaiah 54:4** (Guilty / Ashamed): addressed to Zion as a widow; the "shame of your youth" is not about personal guilt.
- **Proverbs 4:11** (Hopeful / Need direction): the speaker is a parent teaching a child, not God.
